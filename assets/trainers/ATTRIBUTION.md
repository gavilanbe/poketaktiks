# Trainer sprites

The trainer portraits (Professor Oak, the player, Blue, the Kanto Gym Leaders, Giovanni, Team Rocket and the trainer
classes) are Pokémon Showdown's trainer sprites, 80×80 PNG, used unchanged. The dialogue scenes crop them into
portraits and the commander cut-ins draw them large; nothing is edited on disk.

- Source: https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/trainers/<name>.png
- Fetched 2026-09-23, byte-for-byte as served.
